## Warm up first

Spend 5 to 10 minutes on easy movement before you train: marching in place, arm circles, cat-cow, and lighter versions of the exercises you're about to do.

## Increase slowly

Most training injuries come from doing too much too soon. Add a little at a time: a few more reps, one more set, or slightly heavier weight, not all at once.

## Respect old injuries

If you've hurt your back, knees, or shoulders before, tell the quiz. The app will leave out exercises that are commonly a bad idea for that area. A physical therapist can give advice specific to you.

## Recover well

Sleep, rest days, and drinking enough water all help your body repair and lower your injury risk.
