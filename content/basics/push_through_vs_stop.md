## Normal workout discomfort

These are usually fine to work through:

- Muscles burning or feeling tired near the end of a set
- Breathing harder and sweating
- Mild muscle soreness a day or two after a workout

## Stop right away

Stop the exercise and do not push through if you feel:

- Sharp, stabbing, or sudden pain
- Pain in a joint rather than in the muscle
- Numbness, tingling, or pain shooting down an arm or leg
- Dizziness, chest pain, or trouble breathing

If any of these happen, rest and check with a doctor or physical therapist before training that area again.

## A simple rule of thumb

Effort is OK; pain is a signal. If you aren't sure which one you're feeling, stop and choose an easier variation.
