There's a real difference between muscle fatigue (a tired, burning feeling that's a normal part of training) and pain that signals something's wrong (sharp, sudden, or joint-specific pain, or anything that changes your form). General fatigue is usually fine to push through. Sharp or joint pain is a sign to stop and reassess — and if it persists, talk to a doctor or physical therapist.
