## Why rest matters

Rest between sets lets your muscles recover enough to do the next set with good form. Too little rest and your form slips; too much and the workout drags on.

## General ranges

- **Heavier strength work (few reps):** about 1.5 to 3 minutes.
- **Moderate work (8 to 15 reps):** about 60 to 90 seconds.
- **Circuits and endurance work:** about 15 to 45 seconds.

## Rest days

Muscles get stronger while you recover, not while you train. Plan at least one or two full rest days a week, and take an extra one if you feel run down.
