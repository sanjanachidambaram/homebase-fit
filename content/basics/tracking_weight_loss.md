If weight loss is one of your goals, the most reliable way to track progress is consistency, not obsession: weighing yourself at a similar time and frequency, and watching the trend over weeks rather than any single day's number, since weight naturally fluctuates. The CDC notes a safe, sustainable pace is typically about 1-2 pounds per week.
