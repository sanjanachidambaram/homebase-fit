## The scale is noisy

Body weight can swing by a few pounds from day to day because of water, food, and hormones. One weigh-in doesn't tell you much; the trend over weeks does.

## Weigh in the same way

For the most consistent numbers, weigh yourself at the same time of day (for example, first thing in the morning) under the same conditions, a few times a week.

## Look beyond the scale

- **Measurements:** waist, hips, and other tape measurements can change even when weight doesn't.
- **Strength:** lifting more or doing more reps is real progress.
- **How you feel:** energy, sleep, and how your clothes fit all count.

## Use the calendar

Log your weight and measurements in the app's calendar so you can see the trend over time instead of focusing on any single day.
